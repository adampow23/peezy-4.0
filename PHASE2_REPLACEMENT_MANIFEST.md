## 0. Premise corrections and adopted decisions

**LB-4 is recorded as a mistaken committed-source premise, not a redesign driver.** Reviewer 2 reports `functions/taskPlan.js:628-665,717-789` has neither reset tombstones nor `aliases[]`. **LB-2 and LB-6 are confirmed and are load-bearing in the replacement.** `Query.select()` is available on `collectionGroup` queries at the pinned SDK, and the existing mapper and `dispositionContract.js` tolerate unknown contract members. Both are cited as premises in their sections rather than reasserted.

**LB-1, LB-5, LB-7, LB-8 are contradicted and force four scope changes**, all adopted: the coordinator enters scope (D30); `TASK_PLAN_RESET_V2` gains a frozen payload union (D24); the startup barrier moves to `PeezyV1App` (D28); and "mechanical enumeration" is replaced by an explicit frozen pointer list (D26).

True event-driven wake is deferred to named **Phase 2b — Deadline-Scheduled Wake**, with a measurable revisit trigger frozen in §5.2 rather than left to judgment.

**Defect classes.** `MECHANIC` = the design changes. `SPEC` = the design was right and the text was not frozen. `SCOPE` = a path, ownership, or envelope list changes. Seven items are MECHANIC and they are the substance of this repair.
