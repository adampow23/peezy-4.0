Job: When S4 is done, the client owns account deletion end to end and can recover every blocked durable store. `DurableStoreRecoveryCoordinator` is the sole actor of `PeezyAccountDeletion-v1.json` and drives the C2.1–C2.7 state machine (`prepared → data_confirmed → purging → local_detaching → auth_finalize_dispatched → guarding → completed | local_cleared`, plus the nonstaged `remote_unverified` terminal: the linked all-scope handoff with the C2.5 remote-unconfirmed presentation, entering neither `completed` nor `local_cleared`) over S1's `AccountDeletionRemoteProviding` transport and S2/S3's exact wires, dispatching the eight purge owners in the C2.2 order, the preference and telemetry barriers, the journal, the terminal consumption, and the C2.5 completion presentation. `LocalPrivacyPurgeCoordinator` holds the Firestore runtime (`FirestoreRuntimeOwner` replaces `TransitionalFirestoreRuntime`), the client-telemetry authority, the room/media/narration-transfer actor, and the completion-presentation owner. Every durable store (route, handoff, reset, workflow) is classified in the C9.7.3 order into the C9.7.2 blocked-snapshot union and recovered by the C9.7.4 actions through the C9.7.5 replacement sequences with the C9.7.6 item keys and the C9.7.12 attempt keys, and `DurableStoreRecoveryView` overlays those actions and the reset epoch-conflict options over a mountable-while-blocked surface. `ResetOperationRegistry` gains the C9.4.5 `LegacyResetMigrationV1` rows (alias candidates 1–4, inspect flow, APPLYING compare-and-remove of `phase1.pendingRetakeOperation.{uid}`). `TasksStore` and its Home/container consumers (`PeezyMainContainer`, `AppRootView`, `PeezyHomeViewModel`) are namespaced by UID and load token (P1-Q/P1-R), dose state is read only through `DailyDoseLocalStore`, and `TasksStore` carries the C9.3.14 urgent-recovery projection; the task list views are edited only as those consumers require; `TaskDispositionSurface` is the tri-state shared surface; the narration transfer holds actor-issued leases only. Nothing is mounted in `PeezyV1App` and no runtime static is declared: S4 delivers unmounted orchestration proved on seam fakes; it becomes live when S5's conformers and S7's mount land.

Why: The brand promise ("someone in your corner") is defensible only if deletion is complete on the device the user holds, not just on the server, and a store that cannot be read must be recoverable without losing the last copy of user state. S4 is the second deletion slice and the one the user sees.
