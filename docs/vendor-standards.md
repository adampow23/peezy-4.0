# The Peezy standard

Moving day is personal. People hand you their homes, their belongings, and a day that already carries plenty of pressure. Peezy partners show up ready, work with care, and leave customers feeling looked after.

## What customers can count on

- **Honor the arrival window.** If something changes, call early and give a real update.
- **Keep the crew moving steadily.** Work safely, stay focused, and communicate before a delay becomes a surprise.
- **Protect the home and the move.** Use the right equipment, handle belongings with care, and speak up immediately if something is damaged.
- **Charge what was quoted.** Explain any customer-requested scope change before the work continues. Surprise fees are not part of a Peezy move.
- **No day-of price changes.** A confirmed day-of price change is a removal offense.

## How the check-in works

After the move, Peezy asks four factual questions: Did the crew arrive in the window? Did they work steadily? Did anything cost more than quoted? Was anything damaged?

A price or damage report opens a review. It is not treated as confirmed until we check the facts with both sides. Clear communication and useful records help us resolve reviews quickly.

## The accountability ladder

- One confirmed strike means a direct conversation and a clear correction.
- Two confirmed strikes mean a formal warning.
- Three confirmed strikes mean removal from Peezy.
- One confirmed day-of price change means immediate removal.

Reports that are not supported by the facts are dismissed and do not move the ladder.

## The rate card is the promise

Your accepted rate card applies to the move scope Peezy sends: inventory or cubic volume, crew size, drive time, access details, packing status, specialty items, valuation choice, and disclosed surcharges. If the customer changes that scope, pause, explain the difference, and get their agreement before continuing.

Good partners should be easy to trust and easy to recommend. That is the whole standard.
