## Scope statement

The catalog sheet goes live: config-driven flows replace the 39 templated screens, the router becomes data-driven, the approved merges/adds ship as catalog v2, the paywall gate (c) is wired, and the dose freezes. After this spec, adding a vertical is a Firestore write.

Existing-user task migration is OUT OF SCOPE and handled by fiat: test users retake the assessment after reseed. Production has no real users yet — if that's wrong, STOP and report.

## Phase A: FlowDefinition + FlowEngine (CORE)

**READ FIRST:** all 18 files in Tasks/Task Card Components/; three representative templated flows (one 88-line self-service, one ~405-line manage-provider, one Type-3 address flow); TaskFlowRouter.swift; functions/workflowQualifying.js + miniAssessmentWorkflows.js (the orphaned server content — its question data seeds definitions).

1. **`FlowDefinition` model** (Codable): ordered `steps: [FlowStep]` where FlowStep = {id, componentKind, config, branches}. `componentKind` enumerates ONLY what the 18-component kit already renders (title, tiles, decision, businessSearch, confirmAddress, confirmDate, status, summary, info, …as discovered). Branching: step-level `next` overrides keyed by answer value — expressiveness capped at what the 39 templated flows actually use (READ them; do not design beyond).
2. **Firestore collection `flowDefinitions`**, seeded from a new `functions/flowDefinitionsData.json` — generated by transcribing the 39 templated flows' configs (strings, icons, options) and folding in the orphaned WORKFLOW_QUALIFYING questions. The 8 custom flows (insurance ×2, FindMovers, FindCleaners, SetupInternet, SellItems, RemoveItems, RentTruck) are NOT transcribed — they keep their Swift screens this spec.
3. **`FlowEngineView`**: renders a FlowDefinition via the kit, persists answers per step (kills the answer-once ephemeral dict), writes `stage` transitions via TaskActionService, resumes at persisted stage. Answer submission at flow end goes through the existing WorkflowService callable unchanged.
4. Per-flow accessibility ids derive from step ids.

**Acceptance criteria:** for 5 representative migrated flows (validator picks across types): definition-rendered flow is screen-by-screen equivalent to the old Swift screen (side-by-side screenshots); answers persist per step and survive kill/relaunch mid-flow resuming at the same step; final submission payload byte-equivalent to the old flow's (captured diff).

## Phase E: Dose freeze + reflect-backs (PERIPHERY)

1. **Freeze:** DailyDoseEngine computes once per calendar day, persists {date, taskIds} on the user doc, serves the frozen set for the rest of the day; mid-day generations join tomorrow. Done-for-today fires on frozen-set completion.
2. **Reflect-backs:** revive the `inputContext` pathway as a rendered banner on the question FOLLOWING each beat (mechanism option (a) from the Spec 02 report). Beats + LOCKED copy: after pets → "Vet transfer just went on your plan."; after kids → "School and daycare handling — on the plan."; after address pair → "Got both addresses — your plan is taking shape."; after services → "Movers, cleaning, supplies: we'll bring you options — you'll never chase quotes."

**Acceptance criteria:** dose announced as N stays N after a mid-day server-side task insert (evidence: insert + recount); each banner renders in a full run (screenshots).
